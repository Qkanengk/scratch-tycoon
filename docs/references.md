# Reference games

Games with the same core idea (scratch tickets, reinvest the winnings, upgrade) that were looked at before
choosing the art direction and the asset list. Nothing was copied from them; the notes say what each one does
that is worth learning from, and what Scratch Tycoon does differently.

| Game | What it is | Worth borrowing | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Scratch Inc.](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3788420/Scratch_Inc/) | Idle scratch-card game: upgrades, automation, prestige, offline earnings | Clear loop from hand scratching to automation; ticket tiers that look distinct at a glance | Flat 2D presentation with little sense of a physical ticket |
| [Lucky Ticket Tycoon](https://akka11.itch.io/lucky-ticket-tycoon) | Scratch-ticket empire with many ticket types, tools, events, prestige | Tool progression (coin, then better scrapers); each ticket type has its own rule | Many systems unlocked early, which thins out the long game |
| [Scratch Tickets With Your Friends](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5148990/Scratch_Tickets_With_Your_Friends/) | Scratching under survival pressure: rising rent, setup upgrades | Pressure that forces spending decisions (our later rent and tax systems) | First-person room that hides the ticket |
| [Scratchies](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4001240/Scratchies/) | Roguelite scratch-card simulator with perks that bend the odds | Tactile scratching, foil debris, readable symbols | Run-based structure; ours is one long save |
| [Scratch the Ticket](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4626940/Scratch_the_Ticket/) | First-person incremental scratch simulator with cash-out risk | 3D ticket on a table, coin as the tool | Realistic rendering; we go flat toon |
| [Cat Lottery Shop](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3438950/Cat_Lottery_Shop/) | Lottery shop management with scratch cards and customers | Shop props (machine, register) as part of the scene; warm palette | Shop management is out of scope |
| [How Many Tickets Does It Take To Win The Lottery?](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5450860/How_Many_Tickets_Does_It_Take_To_Win_The_Lottery/) | Scratch and reinvest, built around the real odds | Honest odds shown to the player | Single joke, no long progression |

## What this means for Scratch Tycoon's assets

- The ticket is a physical object on a table, seen from above at an angle, with a coin as the scratching tool.
- Each ticket tier has its own paper pattern and symbol family so tiers are recognisable without reading:
  Đồng (fruit machine symbols), Bạc (bầu cua: gourd, crab, fish, rooster, stag), Vàng and Ngọc (numbers),
  Rồng (dragons and bombs).
- Shop props (ticket machine, cash register, prize wheel) dress the table instead of a separate shop screen.
- Scratching throws foil flakes and curls; wins throw coins, stars and confetti. All flat with ink outlines.
